ARC-6

An early concept I’m developing for our university robotics team: a one-metre, six-axis arm with interchangeable grippers and a camera. The plan is to use cycloidal drives and Python control. So far, I’ve used CAD to explore how the parts fit together and how the arm moves. The drives, materials and lifting capacity are still being worked out.

Try it yourself. The final section is a fully interactive model: move the arm by its gripper and use it to pick up and stack blocks.
